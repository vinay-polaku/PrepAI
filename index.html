Your 30-Second Elevator Pitch

Use this script to answer the dreaded: "Tell me about yourself."

Delivery Guidelines

  • Keep it under 150 words: Most people speak at roughly 130-150 words per minute. Aim for around 75-100 words.
  • Hook, Credentials, Value: Start with a strong line, share 1-2 key achievements, and explain why you're a fit for this role specifically.
  • Speak slowly: Natural speaking speed in interviews should feel deliberate. Keep breathing.
  • End with a hook: Conclude by expressing enthusiasm for the team's core problems.
